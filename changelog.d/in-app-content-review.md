- In-app content review replaces the Word review document. Reviewers open `/review` (also linked from Me) to see a queue of every Draft item: 48 learning units, 32 misconceptions, 16 check questions and the curriculum codes on 28 lesson topics (Years 11 and 12 included). The queue filters by type, year level, state, status and search, opens on the years and state the pilot teachers chose, and shows progress overall and by type plus recent activity.
- Each item shows its content, every state's curriculum codes with their official wording, and its history. Reviewers can approve, edit (text, questions, options, answers, feedback and worked solutions, with a maths preview), flag with a comment, comment, or send it back to draft. Approvals are tied to the exact content: an edit, or a later change to the file in the repo, needs approving again. Two reviewers can't overwrite each other: the second gets a "someone has just reviewed this" message.
- Approved items drop the Draft label for every teacher straight away, and reviewer edits show in the app (learning units, misconceptions and check questions).
- The admin can add or remove reviewers by email at `/review/reviewers`. Adding someone also puts them on the pilot list. Denise Halliday is the first reviewer.
