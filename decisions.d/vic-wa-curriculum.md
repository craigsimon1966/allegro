## Victorian and WA curriculum codes for Foundation to Year 10 (27 Nov 2026)

- Victoria: codes and wording come from the VCAA's Word download of Victorian Curriculum F–10 Version 2.0 Mathematics (`VC2-Mathematics.docx`, linked from vic.gov.au/victorian-curriculum-f-10-downloads, updated 17 November 2026). The f10.vcaa.vic.edu.au site was in maintenance, and both hosts block downloads from the build machine, so the file was read in a browser and checked by hash (see docs/vic-wa-curriculum-source.md). Levels A to D (Towards Foundation) and the optional 10A content are not included.
- WA: the SCSA's "Mathematics Scope and sequence of the mandated curriculum content P–10, for implementation in 2026" (Word). The document has no codes, so, as for SACE and TASC, each entry is named the way the document is organised: year level and sub-strand, for example "Year 8 Algebraic techniques" or "Pre-primary Statistics". The entry's text is that cell's mandated content descriptions. Content marked "Year 9 optional" or "Year 10 optional" is left out.
- `content/curriculum/vic-maths.json` and `wa-maths.json` hold only the entries Allegro's units and topics use (140 Victorian codes, 67 WA entries), not the whole curriculum. Adding a new code means extracting it the same way.
- Each unit and topic got its Victorian codes by matching its AC v9 codes to the Victorian code with the same content (Victorian Curriculum 2.0 is adapted from AC v9; numbering differs, for example AC9M7N07 integers is VC2M7N08). WA entries were chosen by sub-strand. Every match is listed in docs/curriculum-todo.md for Denise or Craig to confirm.
- Lesson plans for Victorian and WA teachers show AC v9 plus their own curriculum row, and the AI is given their curriculum's wording for the year (instead of AC v9) when it picks the lesson's focus.
