## 26 Nov 2026: lesson pack as two AI calls

- A real lesson's pack (steps, worksheets and slides in one reply) ran past 6,000 output tokens and was cut off. Rather than raise one limit further (a longer single reply also means a longer wait), the pack is split into two calls run side by side: teacher steps, worksheets and exit ticket (8,000 tokens) and slides (6,000 tokens). The wait is the longer of the two.
- If one half fails, only that half is built from the lesson text, the pack is marked with `plain`, the screen says which part, and the pack is not cached, so the next download tries the AI again.
