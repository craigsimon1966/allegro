## SACE and TASC senior maths (26 Nov 2026)

- SACE and TASC documents have no outcome codes, so Allegro names each entry by the document's own numbering ("Stage 2 Subtopic 4.1", "MTG315123 Outcome 7") rather than making up codes. They live in content/curriculum/senior-maths.json, generated by the import script.
- The same five senior topics serve every state; each state sees a topic only when its course has content for it in that year.
- SACE Stage 1 and TASC Level 2 are treated as Year 11; SACE Stage 2 and TASC Level 3 as Year 12. The NT follows SACE.
- QCAA, SCSA and BSSS sites block automated downloads with a browser check, and the VCAA site was down for maintenance, so Queensland, WA, the ACT and Victoria wait for their official documents.
