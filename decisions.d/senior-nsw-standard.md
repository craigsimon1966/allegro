## Years 11 and 12: NSW Mathematics Standard first (26 Nov 2026)

- Senior lesson topics carry `states` (for example `["NSW"]`) and NSW Stage 6 outcomes instead of AC v9 codes. Plan a lesson shows them for Years 11 and 12 only when the teacher's state has that course (no state counts as NSW, as elsewhere in the demo).
- Year 12 topics list both Standard 1 and Standard 2 outcomes, since the teacher's pathway isn't asked for. The lesson prompt names the course and lists only the outcomes for the chosen year.
- Other states' general courses (VCE General Mathematics, QCAA General Mathematics, SCSA Mathematics Applications, SACE General Mathematics, TASC General Mathematics, BSSS Mathematical Applications) will be added from their official documents in later batches.
