## 26 Nov 2026: teacher situation and days a week

- Craig asked for a situation question with options plus free text. Role stays as its own question; situations are multi-select and imply a role only when no role is chosen. The existing free text box is reused rather than adding a second one.
- "Confident teaching" and "New to" year pickers only show for the two situations they apply to (new year level or stage, harder course), to keep the form short.
- Days a week replaces specific weekdays. Since we don't know which days are off, the first plan spreads learning evenly: 1 day off is Friday, 2 is Tuesday and Friday, 3 is Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 4 is all but Wednesday. The teacher can move any unit to any day. The old weekday list stays readable in the schema only so saved profiles can be counted over (local store version 2).
